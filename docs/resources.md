## 书籍

### 入门书籍

1. **《生物信息学》** - 李霞、雷健波
2. **《R语言实战》** - Robert I. Kabacoff
3. **《Python编程：从入门到实践》** - Eric Matthes

### 进阶书籍

1. **《Bioinformatics and Functional Genomics》** - Jonathan Pevsner
2. **《An Introduction to Statistical Learning》** - James et al.
3. **《The Elements of Statistical Learning》** - Hastie et al.

## 重要文献

### 综述文章

1. **Network Biology**
   - Barabási, A. L., & Oltvai, Z. N. (2004). Network biology: understanding the cell's functional organization. *Nature Reviews Genetics*.

2. **RNA-seq**
   - Conesa, A., et al. (2016). A survey of best practices for RNA-seq data analysis. *Genome Biology*.

3. **Single-cell RNA-seq**
   - Luecken, M. D., & Theis, F. J. (2019). Current best practices in single‐cell RNA‐seq analysis. *Molecular Systems Biology*.
